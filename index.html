Saved data & backups

Entries save automatically on this device, in this browser. The installed Home Screen app stores your logs on this device using IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback. Safari and each installed app icon have separate storage; transfer records with Export / Import. Your workout logs are not sent to GitHub. Storage can still be removed by iOS, device reset, or deleting the app. Export a JSON backup to iCloud Drive regularly.

Auto-save
Weights and reps are saved separately for Set 1 and Set 2. Myo-rep mini-sets, stretched partials, conditioning, stretches, dates, and notes are also stored by week. Once installed and opened online, the app also works offline. No account is required. Save JSON backups to iCloud Drive, especially before reinstalling or changing devices.
